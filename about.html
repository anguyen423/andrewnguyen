About Andrew Nguyen

Hi, I'm Andrew Nguyen — a data-driven problem solver passionate about transforming raw data into meaningful insights that drive smarter decisions and better outcomes. With a strong foundation in data analysis, visualization, and database management, I specialize in helping organizations streamline operations and uncover opportunities through thoughtful, actionable analytics.

Currently, I serve as a Database Manager and Data Analyst at Bishop O’Dowd High School, where I lead data systems strategy and reporting across academic and business departments. My previous role at UCSF Health sharpened my skills in ETL processes, dashboard creation, and performance analysis in a high-paced healthcare environment.

I'm proficient in SQL, Python, Tableau, and Excel, with hands-on experience in PowerSchool, G Suite, ServiceNow, and Cisco's reporting tools. Whether it's managing large-scale databases, building dashboards, or collaborating across teams to optimize workflows, I bring a blend of technical know-how and strategic thinking to every project.

When I’m not diving into data, you can find me coaching youth volleyball as a dedicated mentor and community builder — something I’ve proudly done for over a decade.

I'm excited to bring my experience and curiosity to a new opportunity where I can continue helping teams grow smarter with data.
